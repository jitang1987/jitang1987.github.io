About

Tang Ji holds a PhD in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Auckland. His research focuses on Cognitive Digital Twin (CDT) for Smart Manufacturing, with particular interests in data interoperability, industrial communication and intelligent manufacturing systems.

His work explores Digital Twin technologies, industrial communication protocols, AI-enabled methods and Learning Factories for Smart Manufacturing and Industry 4.0 applications.

Research

Cognitive Digital Twin Smart Manufacturing Digital Twin Industry 4.0 Industrial Interoperability

Cognitive Digital Twin

Development of intelligent Digital Twin systems that integrate physical manufacturing assets, digital models, industrial data and AI-enabled capabilities.

Smart Manufacturing

Research on connected and intelligent manufacturing systems, including Cyber-Physical Production Systems and Industry 4.0 technologies.

Industrial Interoperability

Integration of industrial communication protocols and information models to support reliable data exchange across manufacturing systems.

Learning Factories

Research on Learning Factory architectures, implementation testbeds and their role in Smart Manufacturing education, research and industrial engagement.
