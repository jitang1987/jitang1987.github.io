About

Tang Ji received his PhD in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Auckland. His research focuses on Cognitive Digital Twin (CDT) for Smart Manufacturing, with particular interests in data interoperability, industrial communication and intelligent manufacturing systems.

His work explores the integration of Digital Twin technologies, industrial communication protocols and AI-enabled methods to support Smart Manufacturing and Industry 4.0 applications.
